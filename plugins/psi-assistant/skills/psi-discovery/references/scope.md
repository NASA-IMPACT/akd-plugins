# Scope

## Purpose
- Scientific knowledge and data assistant for NASA’s Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI).
- PSI provides open access to data from microgravity and ground-based physical sciences investigations spanning multiple disciplines (e.g., materials science, fluid physics, combustion, fundamental physics, soft matter).
- Supports semantic search and retrieval across datasets, documents, and publications.
- Performs summarization and structured extraction of scientific content.
- Supports cross-dataset and cross-domain analysis.
- Produces structured outputs (e.g., metadata, comparisons, insights).
- Improves data discoverability, contextualization, and usability in PSI-like repositories.
- Positioning: research augmentation system (information retrieval + knowledge synthesis + context expansion) with human-in-the-loop validation.

## Users
### Primary users (roles)
- Researchers / Scientists
- Graduate students and advanced learners
- Subject-matter experts (SMEs)
- Data scientists / engineers

### Secondary users (roles)
- Educators / students
- Data curators / administrators

## User expertise
- Mixed expertise levels:
  - Experts (primary)
  - Intermediate users (graduate students, engineers)
- Must support technical depth with optional simplification.

## Tasks the agent supports
### Retrieval & search
- Semantic + keyword search across datasets, publications, and reports.
- Filtering by parameters, domain, and metadata.

### Extraction & structuring
- Extract variables, parameters, and metadata.
- Generate structured outputs.

### Summarization & comparison
- Summarize methods and findings.
- Compare across studies.
- Identify trends and relationships.

### Insight support
- Identify related work.
- Identify cross-domain links.
- Identify research gaps.

## Current workflow (as-is)
- Overall workflow is manual and fragmented across tools/platforms.
- Highly dependent on keyword search, manual reading, and manual comparison.
- Define research question.
- Search repositories manually.
- Download/read datasets & papers.
- Extract and interpret data manually.
- Perform comparisons/analysis locally.

## Pain points / bottlenecks
- Inefficient search and discovery.
- Manual literature/data review.
- Difficulty identifying relevance.
- Inconsistent data formats.
- Time-intensive comparison and synthesis.

## Human-controlled decisions
- Scientific interpretation.
- Final conclusions.
- Dataset selection and validation.
- Research direction / hypothesis acceptance.
- Credibility and quality judgment.
- Agent role is assistive, not authoritative.

## Success criteria
- Reduced time spent on search, analysis, and synthesis.
- Improved relevance and accuracy of results.
- Increased data usability.
- Reduced manual effort.
- Users validate outputs.
